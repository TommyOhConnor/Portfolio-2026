Privacy Policy

Effective August 20, 2026

Job Alert is a direct job-search notification service operated by Thomas OConnor. This policy explains how information is handled when SMS alerts are enabled.

Information collected

The service may collect a mobile phone number, SMS consent and preference records, delivery and opt-out status, and limited technical records needed to operate the alert service.

How it is used

Information is used only to send requested notifications about newly discovered job opportunities, maintain consent and opt-out preferences, troubleshoot delivery, and protect the service from misuse.

SMS program

Message frequency varies based on whether qualifying job openings are discovered. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance.

Data sharing

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Information may be processed by service providers, including Twilio, only as necessary to deliver and support the requested messages. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect the security of the service.

Retention and security

Information is retained only as long as needed to operate the alerts, document consent and opt-out requests, meet legal obligations, and resolve delivery issues. Reasonable safeguards are used to protect it.

Your choices

You may stop SMS alerts at any time by replying STOP. For questions, assistance, or a request concerning your information, reply HELP or email me@tommyoconnor.com.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
