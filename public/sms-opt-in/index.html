Thomas OConnor · Career Ops Alerts

SMS Opt-In

Last updated August 20, 2026

Career Ops Alerts is a direct job-search notification program operated by Thomas OConnor. It sends requested automated alerts when a newly discovered role meets the subscriber's selected Focus Now or Investigate criteria. The program does not advertise third-party services or send marketing messages on behalf of third parties or affiliates.

How to opt in

From the mobile number that will receive the alerts, text START to +1 (928) 492-6911.

By texting START, you expressly consent to receive recurring automated job-search notification messages from Thomas OConnor Career Ops Alerts at the number used to opt in. This is the only opt-in method used for this program.

You will receive this confirmation: “Thomas OConnor Career Ops Alerts: You are subscribed to automated job-search alerts. Messages are sent only when a qualifying job is found; message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to cancel.”

What you will receive

Messages are sent only when a newly discovered job opening meets the configured relevance threshold. An alert may include the company, job title, fit score and category, available salary information, connection level, and a direct link to the original job posting.

Required disclosures

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchasing any product or service.

Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for assistance, or email me@tommyoconnor.com.

Privacy and terms

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Review the Privacy Policy and SMS Terms & Conditions before opting in.
